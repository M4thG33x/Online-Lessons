  • Determine the area of a surface defined by a 2-variable function.

A common application of double integrals is computing the area of a surface defined by a function of two variables.

For a surface \(z=f(x,y)\) defined with continuous partial derivatives over a region \(R\) in the \(xy\)-plane, we can compute the area of the surface over \(R\) using the formula below.

\[A = \underset{R}{\mathop \iint} \, \sqrt{\left[f_x(x,y)\right]^2+\left[f_y(x,y)\right]^2+1} \, dA\]
